Precision Machinery & Capabilities

Equipped to deliver high-accuracy panel fabrication, custom dimensional envelopes, and reliable ingress protection.

Cutting Technology

CNC Fiber Laser Profiling

High-speed fiber laser cutting delivers rapid, micron-accurate contours, cutouts, and hole arrays directly from CAD geometry with zero mechanical deformation or tooling wear.

  • Tight Tolerances: Consistent accuracy for seamless fitment
  • Materials Handled: CRCA, Galvanized Iron (GI), Stainless Steel (SS304/SS316)
  • Nitrogen Assist: Oxide-free edge finish ensuring superior weld and paint adhesion
  • Automated Nesting: Optimizing raw material utilization and cost efficiency
Forming Precision

Multi-Axis CNC Hydraulic Bending

High-tonnage computerized press brakes shape complex sheet profiles with digital angle compensation. Double-fold return flanges, stiffener ribs, and interlocking gasket grooves are bent with mathematical consistency.

  • Computerized Backgauges: Multi-axis positioning for complex multi-bend sequences
  • Angle Consistency: Crowning compensation preventing longitudinal bow
  • Flange Stiffness: Engineered return bends for high structural door rigidity
  • Gasket Recess Channels: Precision formed channels for secure gasket compression
Assembly Rigor

Precision Welding & Structural Assembly

Structural corner pillars, modular cross-members, and enclosure shells are joined with inert gas shielded MIG, TIG, and projection stud welding. Welds are dressed flush for an aesthetic, high-strength industrial finish.

  • Argon Shielded TIG/MIG: Clean, non-porous seams with minimal heat distortion
  • Stud Welding: Capacitor discharge copper-coated grounding studs
  • Seam Finishing: Flush grinding and edge linishing preventing sharp burrs
  • Rigid Modular Framing: High load-bearing capacity for heavy busbars and switchgear
Sealing Science

Formed-in-Place PU Foamed Gasketing

Continuous polyurethane (PU) foamed gaskets are dispensed directly into enclosure door channels. Unlike jointed strip rubber, continuous poured foam creates a jointless barrier with excellent compression recovery.

  • Jointless Sealing: Seamless corner transitions eliminating moisture ingress paths
  • High Compression Elasticity: 100% elastic recovery after repeated door latchings
  • Protection Standards: Ready for IP55, IP65, and IP66 ingress protection
  • Chemical & Temperature Resistance: Durable across industrial plant environments
