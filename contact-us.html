Inquiry FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding RFQs, plant visits, and custom fabrication consultations.

How fast will I receive a response to my RFQ submission?

Our technical estimation desk acknowledges inquiries immediately and provides detailed engineering quotations within 24 to 48 business hours upon reviewing your CAD drawings or technical requirements.

Can clients schedule a factory visit to inspect ongoing fabrication?

Yes. Clients, panel builders, and engineering consultants are welcome to visit our plant in Ramnagar, Khed-Shivapur road, Pune, Maharashtra between 8:30 AM and 7:30 PM, Monday through Saturday.

Does SKS support single-unit custom prototypes as well as volume runs?

Yes. We support custom one-off prototype development for pilot projects, as well as recurring scheduled production runs for switchboard builders and OEM equipment manufacturers.
