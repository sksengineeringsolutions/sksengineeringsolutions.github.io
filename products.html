Product FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details about our customized electrical enclosures, modular cabinets, and stainless steel product lines.

Can floor-standing cabinets be customized to non-standard dimensions?

Yes. Every floor-standing enclosure, modular bay, and wall-mounted box is customized to exact client height, width, and depth specifications, including custom plinth heights, gland plate positions, and sub-panel mounting arrangements.

What applications are your Stainless Steel (SS304/SS316) enclosures designed for?

Our SS304 and SS316 enclosures are engineered for pharmaceutical cleanrooms, chemical processing units, food and beverage processing lines, and aggressive coastal or outdoor industrial environments requiring high corrosion resistance.

What is included with your outdoor feeder pillar enclosures?

Outdoor feeder pillars feature a slopped canopy rain-hood, louvered ventilation panels with internal wire mesh to prevent insect entry, IP55 sealing gaskets, three-point lockable doors, and anti-corrosive base plinths.

What is your typical turnaround time for quotation and fabrication?

Standard engineering quotation estimates are provided within 24 to 48 business hours upon receiving 2D/3D CAD drawings. Fabrication lead times depend on batch volume and design complexity, typically 7 to 15 working days.
