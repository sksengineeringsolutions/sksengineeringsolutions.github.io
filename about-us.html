Our Profile

Precision Engineering Under Shinde Groups

SKS Engineering Solutions operates as a key manufacturing enterprise under the prestigious parent group, Shinde Groups. Located strategically in Pune, Maharashtra, we specialize in customized fabrication, electrical panel fabrication, industrial enclosures, and client-specific engineering solutions.

With 2 Years of Experience in Panel Fabrication, our enterprise is built upon the foundational principles of dimensional accuracy, structural robustness, and client-centric engineering. We serve electrical panel builders, automation system integrators, original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), and infrastructure contractors who demand uncompromising craftsmanship.

"Our core operational focus is dedicated to the fabrication of empty structural enclosures. We do not compete with electrical panel builders or system integrators; rather, we empower them with high-accuracy, defect-free sheet metal structures that accelerate assembly and field deployment."

Operational Philosophy

Our Engineering & Manufacturing Approach

A disciplined, client-aligned fabrication framework tailored for industrial panel enclosures.

APPROACH 01
Design Fidelity
📐

Client-Focused Customization

We do not force clients into rigid catalog dimensions. Every enclosure is engineered around your internal component layout, cable entry directions, busbar paths, and mounting tolerances.

APPROACH 02
Tolerance Control
⚙️

Quality-Focused Manufacturing

From prime-grade certified raw sheet inspection (CRCA, GI, SS304) to high-speed fiber laser cutting and multi-axis CNC bending, each stage adheres to strict quality checksheets.

APPROACH 03
Sealing Rigor
🛡️

Ingress Protection Compliance

We utilize continuous formed-in-place polyurethane (PU) foamed gasketing along door returns and gland covers to ensure genuine IP55 and IP65 dust and moisture resistance.

Transparent Partnership
🤝

Zero Conflict Collaboration

We supply empty, structurally certified enclosure shells. System integrators and panel builders can rest assured that their client relationships and proprietary engineering remain 100% confidential.
