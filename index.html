Verified Fabrication Capabilities

Cutting Precision

CNC Fiber Laser Profiling

High-speed fiber laser cutting delivers rapid, micron-accurate contours, cutouts, and hole arrays directly from CAD geometry with zero mechanical deformation.

  • Tight tolerance accuracy for seamless fitment
  • Nitrogen assist for oxide-free weld edges
  • Prime CRCA, GI, and SS304/SS316 sheet handling
Forming Precision

Multi-Axis CNC Hydraulic Bending

High-tonnage computerized press brakes shape complex sheet profiles with digital angle compensation. Double-fold return flanges and interlocking gasket grooves are bent with mathematical consistency.

  • Multi-axis backgauging for complex folding sequences
  • Engineered return bends for maximum door rigidity
  • Precision formed channels for gasket sealing
