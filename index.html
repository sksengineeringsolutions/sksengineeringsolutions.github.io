Company Profile

Engineered for Sheet Metal Excellence

"At SKS Engineering Solutions, our mission is to deliver durable, high-precision sheet metal enclosures, control desks, and custom junction boxes tailored for panel builders, system integrators, and industrial plants. With advanced CNC laser cutting, tight-tolerance hydraulic bending, continuous PU foamed gasketing, and automated 7-tank powder coating, we provide certified, zero-defect enclosure structures delivered strictly on schedule."

Operating from our specialized manufacturing facility in Pune, Maharashtra, we engineer empty and modular enclosure structures built to withstand harsh industrial environments. Whether you require standard modular enclosures, outdoor rainproof feeder pillars, or custom-punched stainless steel console desks, our engineering team manufactures to your exact 3D SolidWorks and CAD specifications.
